## Count definitions

The confirmed key-problem count is conservative: it includes a changed answer set with a supported alternative, or an explicitly outdated premise that removes the original key’s unique validity. Every unresolved item has a per-choice rationale and references. No source key is used to score an unresolved question.

Topic labels are manually reviewed study categories, not official blueprint weights. The 11 similar-prompt pairs remain separate because source wording, options, or figures differ; there are no exact complete-question duplicates after whitespace and native-figure checks.
